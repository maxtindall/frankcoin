Honest cautions

Uncapped mining fights a stable market. Every proof mints new supply — constant sell-pressure and dilution. Most tradeable memecoins are fixed-supply for this reason. The Dogecoin-style tail and a deep pool are in tension; the emission rate is a real design decision, not a detail.

This is real money and real exposure. Providing liquidity commits capital at market risk. A public tradeable token carries securities and regulatory questions that need real counsel. Today frankcoin is on devnet — worth nothing. Nothing here is an offer, a sale, a security, or financial advice.
